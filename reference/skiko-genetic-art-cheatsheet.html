Skiko & Genetic Art Quick Reference

Cheat sheet and quick-reference guide for standalone Skiko desktop rendering and (1+1) Hill Climber evolutionary art.

1. Essential CLI Commands

ActionCommand

3. The (1+1) Hill Climber Mathematical Engine

ConceptMathematical Formula / Definition
Quadratic Bézier Curve\( B(t) = (1 - t)^2 P_0 + 2(1 - t)t P_c + t^2 P_1, \quad t \in [0, 1] \)
L2 Squared Error (SSD)\( \text{Loss} = \sum_{p \in \text{pixels}} \big((R_{\text{cand}} - R_{\text{tgt}})^2 + (G_{\text{cand}} - G_{\text{tgt}})^2 + (B_{\text{cand}} - B_{\text{tgt}})^2\big) \)
Selection Rule (with Neutral Drift)Accept mutant if \(\text{Error}(\text{Mutant}) \le \text{Error}(\text{Champion})\), else reject.
