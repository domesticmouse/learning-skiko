# Implemented Phase 2 Advanced Extension Lessons (Lessons 0006–0011)

The complete suite of six advanced extension lessons planned in the curriculum roadmap has been fully authored and integrated into `./lessons/`, expanding the course from foundational engine deconstruction into cutting-edge evolutionary art engineering across three specialized tracks:

1. **Track 1: Algorithmic Scaling & Concurrency**
   - **Lesson 0006 (`0006-multicore-island-model.html`)**: Multi-core worker pools, $(1+\lambda)$ evolution strategy, demographic isolation in demes, asynchronous ring champion migration, and interactive 4-island canvas simulation.
   - **Lesson 0007 (`0007-adaptive-annealing-pyramids.html`)**: Simulated annealing temperature cooling schedules ($\sigma(t)$), Rechenberg's 1/5th empirical success rule, coarse-to-fine Gaussian image pyramids ($64\times 64 \to 128\times 128 \to 256\times 256$), and interactive annealing canvas.

2. **Track 2: Fitness Models & Perceptual Aesthetics**
   - **Lesson 0008 (`0008-perceptual-fitness-cielab-sobel.html`)**: Human color vision perception, CIE $L^*a^*b^*$ color space, Euclidean $\Delta E^*_{ab}$ distance, precomputed 3D lookup tables (LUTs) for zero-allocation performance, Sobel $3\times 3$ gradient edge saliency convolution kernels, and interactive filter comparator.
   - **Lesson 0009 (`0009-parsimony-pressure-style-constraints.html`)**: Combating genetic bloat and micro-stroke clutter, regularized multi-objective fitness formulation ($F = E + \alpha N + \beta A$), Pareto dominance frontiers, artistic style constraints (Impressionist, Minimalist, Directional flow), and interactive parsimony playground.

3. **Track 3: Chromosome Expressiveness & Rendering**
   - **Lesson 0010 (`0010-diverse-vector-primitives.html`)**: Expanding beyond uniform quadratic Béziers to cubic Bézier splines (two control handles with inflection points), pressure-tapered calligraphy paths, translucent convex polygon meshes, Skia gradient shaders (`Shader.makeLinearGradient`), polymorphic `Gene` Kotlin architectures, and interactive primitive sandbox.
   - **Lesson 0011 (`0011-svg-export-timelapse-capture.html`)**: Infinite vector scalability, W3C SVG XML serialization (`<path d="M... Q...">`), deterministic checkpointing (`.gene`), logarithmic frame dumping, compiling 4K 60 FPS videos via FFmpeg CLI, and interactive in-browser SVG exporter and time-lapse scrubber.
